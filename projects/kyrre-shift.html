FURNITURE DESIGN — 2026

Kyrre Shift

Summary

Stool and side table in one. Designed for flexible, space-saving homes.

Focus

Multifunctional furniture · small space living · stackable design

Role

Designer

Tools

Rhino · KeyShot · Adobe CC · Vizcom

Overview

Kyrre Shift is a reinterpretation of IKEA’s KYRRE stool designed to expand its everyday functionality. By introducing a reversible geometry, the object works both as a seat and as a small side table while remaining stackable and compact.

Problem

As living spaces become smaller, furniture must serve multiple purposes without increasing visual or spatial clutter. Traditional stools often fulfill a single function, limiting their usefulness in compact homes.

Solution

Kyrre Shift transforms the KYRRE stool into a reversible piece that works as both a seat and a side table, while remaining stackable. The design expands functionality without altering the simplicity of the original object.

Next steps

Further iterations will refine proportions and structural details, followed by manufacturing validation and user testing to evaluate stability, usability, and stackability. Future development would explore scalable production and integration within existing furniture systems.
